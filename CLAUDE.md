# Megasolar

Megasolar is a solar energy company. This repository holds its brand: the chosen logo, the brand kit, and the scripts that build them. `README.md` explains the layout and how to rebuild.

## The chosen brand (decided 17 November 2026)

Use this brand for anything made for Megasolar. Do not redesign it or offer alternatives unless asked.

- **Logo:** the files in `brand/logo/`. Use them as they are; never redraw, recolor, stretch or rearrange them. `brand/archive/logo-v1/` is the earlier logo and is not for new work.
- **Colors:** Ink `#0C1014`, Solar Yellow `#FFD12E`, Paper `#F7F8F5`, in about a 60 / 30 / 10 balance. Yellow is a fill and a signal, never text on white.
- **Type:** Saira Expanded SemiBold for headlines and caps labels, Geist for interface and body text, Geist Mono for numbers and data.
- **Shape:** every slant leans one across for every three up, like the panels in the symbol.
- **Tagline:** "Solar, run like software."

The full usage rules are in `design-system/project/README.md`, and every color, type style, spacing step and radius is in `design-system/project/tokens.json`. Read those before designing or writing anything branded.

## Rules for work in this repository

- Nothing here records facts about the business: what it sells, prices, numbers, client or staff names. Ask for them or leave a marked placeholder such as `[Client name]`. Never invent them.
- Run the build scripts from the repository root. Steps are in `README.md`.
