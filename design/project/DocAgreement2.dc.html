3.Training and commitment

3.1
The Company is paying for the Foreman to attend [course name] run by [training provider] from [start date] to [end date]. It pays the course fees, materials, transport and lodging, shown by receipts and capped at ₱[amount] (the "Training Cost"). It also pays his daily rate for the training days.
3.2
In return, the Foreman commits to work on the Company's projects for [18] months after the training ends.
3.3
If within that period he resigns, refuses projects without a valid reason, abandons a project or is dismissed for a just cause, he repays the unserved share of the Training Cost: the Training Cost, multiplied by the months left, divided by [18]. He pays within 30 days of the Company's written computation, or in instalments over up to [6] months if he asks.
3.4
He owes nothing if the Company ends his engagement without a just cause, offers him no project for [60] days in a row, or fails to pay him on time, or if he stops because of illness or injury.

4.Loyalty and confidentiality

4.1
While this Agreement is in force, the Foreman does not sell, install or service solar power systems for himself or for anyone else in [cities or provinces the Company serves] (the "Territory"). Between projects he may take any other kind of work, and temporary solar work for others once the Company has offered him no project for [30] days. He passes every solar inquiry he receives to the Company.
4.2
For [12] months after he leaves, the Foreman does not:
(a)
do solar work for the Company's clients or for people it has given a quotation;
(b)
recruit the Company's crew or staff; or
(c)
run his own solar installation business in the Territory. This last point does not apply if the Company ended his engagement without a just cause or had no work for him.
