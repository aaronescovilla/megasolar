Graphic language
The lean
Every slant leans one across for every three up, like the panels in the symbol. Meters, tags and photo crops follow it.
The array
Repeat the panel on a grid to build patterns, meters and charts. Lit cells are Solar Yellow.
The pulse
The line in the symbol is output over a day: up after sunrise, down by dusk. Two days draw an M.
Supergraphic crops
Ink on Solar. Covers, signage, launch moments.
Solar on Graphite. Hero areas and section openers.
Tone on tone. Quiet texture behind text.
Mist on Paper. Documents and light pages.
